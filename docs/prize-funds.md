# Protected prize funds and replenishment (T15)

The manager's **Prize funds** workspace reads current purpose evidence, the full promised daily/weekly obligation, every fixed replenishment occurrence, actual settlement state and audit history. Only current campaign managers can read or request a recheck, including exact retries. Platform administrator access elsewhere does not grant this financial manager operation. Public waiting creatives, creator intake, locked rules, advertising allocation and commission records retain their existing flows.

`promisedPrizes` uses T14's actual Chicago periods and counts every complete day and Monday–Sunday week wholly contained in the locked competition dates. Opening/closing partial periods have no prize obligation. With no finalization/payment release producer yet, the entire promised obligation remains protected. Future replenishment, fundraising cash and advertising cash are never projected as already-funded prizes. T09's current read and paid guard consume this projection in addition to their existing independent validation/provenance/release gates. A generic funding proof cannot replace it. Purpose producer/expiry/settlement changes schedule durable blocker reevaluation; direct reads independently revalidate current inputs and expiry.

`recordTrustedPrizeEvidence` is a typed, nonregistered producer seam. There is no application operation for a manager to supply a balance, approve a route or mark a transfer paid. Evidence binds nonprofit, campaign, locked rules, current readiness/account scope, account, route, beneficiary, fundraising source and prize destination, immutable transport/credential digest, reviewed manager, authority revision, exact fixed amount, ordered observation, expiration and retained redacted source reference. Gross donation, current commission, all source/fee ledger observations, refund-read generation/outcome and approved/paid commission facts form the financial revision. A new donation, fee, refund read or authority/scope revision invalidates older evidence without changing original gross or locked rules. Unknown balances, verified negative availability, unfinished/held refund reads, revoked approval, stale evidence and conflicting settlement remain held.

Balances use disjoint purposes. `fundraisingCents` is verified campaign-purpose cash after provider-reflected fees/refunds and source transfer holds; it excludes advertising and prize cash. Creator earnings, refund holds and other outstanding fundraising obligations explicitly identify protection not already deducted from that cash observation. The creator reserve cannot understate the current unpaid commission/approval obligation: per creator/account/route it protects the larger of earned unpaid commission and approved unpaid obligations, avoiding overlapping protection twice. Advertising cash and advertising obligations are displayed separately and never enter the replenishment source calculation. No nonprofit or campaign can supply another's funds.

Canonical accepted pending/requires_action refund observations also establish a minimum reserve, once per account/refund identity. All refund observations enter the financial revision. Exact account/refund/amount identities in `reflectedRefunds` exclude only holds already deducted by the provider from the cash observation; the remaining minimum must be covered by `refundHoldCents`. Foreign, duplicate or changed-amount reflection is rejected. Failed/canceled resolution releases the minimum only after a coherent canonical read; unknown/conflicting/incomplete reads hold the whole projection.

Two reflection sets preserve both sides of settlement. `sourceReflectedTransfers` lists exact previously dispatched journal identities whose source debit/hold is already reflected in the cash observation; other journal amounts remain reserved across fresh snapshots. `reflectedTransfers` lists only settled identities already included in the destination prize balance and requires their source reflection too. Both sets retain history and exact account/route scope. A pending or unknown source-reflected hold removes no second source amount and creates no prize credit. Only independently confirmed settlement credits the prize purpose, once. Missing/failed lookup, HTTP rejection, malformed response or changed transport never proves absence and never authorizes a replacement transfer.

Each campaign creation persists all weekly occurrences from its locked amount, weekday and Chicago clock. An unambiguous occurrence's advance due time is execution minus exactly 86,400,000 ms, including DST. Existing civil-time convention holds a nonexistent or ambiguous clock with its exact date/time for review; it selects neither a shifted time nor a fold. Scheduled due time and actual check time are retained separately. Setup after a due check or first observation at/after execution records an honest missed check and warning, never historical proof. Ordinary scheduler latency retains its actual timestamp. Production internal jobs and run-owned clock controls use the same transactional occurrence/claim/settlement functions.

At execution, the whole fixed amount must be available after obligations and journal reservations. A shortage transfers nothing, records scheduled amount/time, observed availability and shortfall, creates the existing T08 manager-only incident/outbox and schedules the next check one elapsed hour later. Failed/unknown email remains visible under T08 and never claims delivery. Duplicate/reordered/concurrent jobs cannot create another transfer for the same occurrence. A durable immutable journal precedes dispatch; a current authority/revision/account/route/transport fence precedes the POST. Unknown/pending outcomes reconcile by GET against the original identity only. Current system reconciliation can learn an original settlement after the approving manager loses access; revoked callers cannot read/retry. Late observations are retained; exact settlement retries cannot double credit. Restart and missed jobs retain obligations, reservations and reconciliation identity.

The shared signed HTTP transport and owned loopback peer use an explicitly controlled transfer contract. They are not Stripe transfer acceptance or actual financial approval. Every production dispatch is held pending the validated #66/#20/#21 purpose, eligibility, source/destination, route, financial-read/settlement and transport contract. No real funds, credentials, provider account actions, emails, advertising or deployment were used. Replenishment creates neither winners nor creator prize payments; T16/T17/T18 retain those responsibilities. T24/T26 retain advertising funding and full-run advertising coverage. Actual financial availability producers must validate cash/obligation reflection semantics before binding this seam; fixture values cannot be promoted to production authority.
