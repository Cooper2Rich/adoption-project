# Campaign finance read APIs: balances and advertising payment activity

Research date: 2026-11-09. Evidence for [Verify provider APIs for campaign balances and advertising payment activity](https://github.com/Cooper2Rich/adoption-project/issues/19), child of [Plan Meta and TikTok nonprofit campaign accounts and funding](https://github.com/Cooper2Rich/adoption-project/issues/9).

## Scope and verdict

Slander MUST programmatically read provider available balances AND advertising payment activity. Dashboard access, exports, fundraising totals and donation-only feeds cannot satisfy this. Initial manual Wallet setup remains a proposal, not an accepted workflow. Scope: US Slander and separate US nonprofits, USD, initially one advertising campaign per nonprofit with a separate provider-held advertising balance, primarily public advertising crowdfunding plus optional client contributions. Slander manages ads and an approved automatic payment mechanism. Provider, collection merchant, account owner and payer remain human decisions. Operating software does not require Slander to own the money or card.

Evidence notation: **D** current first-party documentation; **S** first-party schema/generated SDK (surface, not access promise); **I** proposed design/inference; **U** unestablished/inaccessible; **A** account-specific approval required. No credentials, authenticated finance calls, account setup, financial operations, provider contact, ads or implementation occurred.

## Stripe Connect + Issuing: reads and balance semantics

**D:** Permitted server credentials plus Stripe-Account: acct_... target v1 reads to the connected nonprofit; omitting it targets the platform ([authentication](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/authentication)). [Key guidance](https://docs.stripe.com/keys) separates server permissions from publishable keys and human roles. Exact restricted-key read grants for each selected product/account require program verification; no invented OAuth scopes or claim that generic access enables Issuing.

| Authenticated read | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| GET /v1/balance | Top-level available/pending are payments COLLECTION funds. issuing.available is card-spend funds for this product. connect_reserved is separate. Read correct context/currency; do not sum them into spend capacity. [Object](https://docs.stripe.com/api/balance/balance_object?query=), [retrieve](https://docs.stripe.com/api/balance/balance_retrieve). |
| GET /v1/issuing/cards and /v1/issuing/cards/{id} | Stable card ID, cardholder, replacement history and account context, rather than last four alone. [Cards](https://docs.stripe.com/api/issuing/cards). |
| GET /v1/issuing/authorizations and /v1/issuing/authorizations/{id} | Pending approvals/holds and lifecycle, card/status/created filters; iauth IDs, approved amount/currency, merchant, request history and balance-transaction links. [List](https://docs.stripe.com/api/issuing/authorizations/list), [object](https://docs.stripe.com/api/issuing/authorizations/object). |
| GET /v1/issuing/transactions and /v1/issuing/transactions/{id} | Captures/refunds, not pending approvals. ipi ID, card/cardholder, nullable authorization, balance_transaction, currency/amount and dispute. Filters card/cardholder/created/type. [List](https://docs.stripe.com/api/issuing/transactions/list), [object](https://docs.stripe.com/api/issuing/transactions/object). |
| GET /v1/balance_transactions and /v1/balance_transactions/{id} | Ledger fee/net/source/availability/category, including holds/releases, Issuing transactions, fees, topups/transfers/reversals. Join IDs with product/account context; each entry is not a new expense. [History](https://docs.stripe.com/api/balance_transactions/list). |
| GET /v1/issuing/disputes and /v1/issuing/disputes/{id} | Outgoing-card disputes, distinct from donor-payment disputes. [Disputes](https://docs.stripe.com/api/issuing/disputes). |

**D:** [Authorization lifecycle](https://docs.stripe.com/issuing/purchases/authorizations): approved amount is deducted from Issuing availability and held; capture releases/closes the hold, reversal/expiry restores availability. Status semantics depend on version, and expiry can precede late capture. Do not automatically subtract outstanding holds again from issuing.available.

**D:** [Capture/refund lifecycle](https://docs.stripe.com/issuing/purchases/transactions) documents hold release plus capture debit, forced captures without authorization, and refunds with absent/inexact original-authorization linkage. Controls do not prevent capture of previously approved purchases. Capture timing is not an advertising-liability freshness guarantee; released holds do not prove merchant debt disappeared.

**D:** [Event catalog](https://docs.stripe.com/api/events/types) includes issuing_authorization.created/updated, issuing_transaction.created/updated and issuing_dispute.created/updated/closed/funds_reinstated/funds_rescinded. issuing_authorization.request is separate synchronous explicit-opt-in authorization control, not a universal ledger. [balance.available](https://docs.stripe.com/api/balance) does not fire for negative transactions and cannot be the sole finance signal.

**D/I:** v1 lists use starting_after/ending_before, has_more and supported filters. Exhaust pages and persist IDs; overlap creation windows for captures/ledger entries and re-retrieve known pending authorizations/disputes to recover later changes. [Webhooks](https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks) can be duplicated/out of order; live automatic retries last up to three days, manual resend up to 15 days in Dashboard/30 days via CLI. [Events API](https://docs.stripe.com/api/events/list) retains only 30 days. Events trigger reconciliation, not permanent history or atomic snapshots. Maintain local history and backfill authoritative objects/lists.

**A:** [Issuing with Connect](https://docs.stripe.com/issuing/connect) has account/capability requirements, not approval for Slander. [Connected funding](https://docs.stripe.com/issuing/connect/funding) requires Balance Transfer API private-beta access for payments-to-Issuing transfers; US bank pull requires verification/authorization/settlement, and US push funding is gated. A collection balance cannot bypass this. A nonprofit's Issuing balance can align with its sole campaign initially; multiple cards do not create separate future campaign balances.

## Financial accounts: current v2 preview versus v1 Treasury

**D/A:** [Treasury for platforms](https://docs.stripe.com/treasury/connect) currently lists US private preview and sales access; it labels financial-account-backed cards private preview too. Public docs do not grant Slander access. Standalone business financial accounts are not automatically delegated multi-nonprofit platform accounts.

**D:** [Financial-account guide](https://docs.stripe.com/treasury/connect/account-management/financial-accounts) distinguishes storage from payments accounts. Storage has distinct balances and USD capabilities money_manager.business_storage.inbound.usd / outbound.usd. Payments-account private preview represents the existing payments balance, not additional cash. Confirm actual account configuration and card program.

**D:** GET /v2/money_management/financial_accounts and /v2/money_management/financial_accounts/{id}: Bearer credentials, supported preview version (current examples 2026-10-28.preview), connected Stripe-Context. [Endpoints](https://docs.stripe.com/api/v2/money-management/financial-accounts?api-version=2026-10-28.preview), [retrieve](https://docs.stripe.com/api/v2/money-management/financial-accounts/retrieve?api-version=2026-10-28.preview). Response has balance.available/inbound_pending/outbound_pending by currency/value; outbound pending is already unspendable. The guide's illustrative payments example instead uses balances with a different shape. **U:** resolve that documentation discrepancy against the approved version/schema before implementation, not as a tested contract.

**S/U:** v2 transaction-reference fetch failed. Official generated [service](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stripe/stripe-python/master/stripe/v2/money_management/_transaction_service.py) establishes GET /v2/money_management/transactions and /{id}; [list params](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stripe/stripe-python/master/stripe/params/v2/money_management/_transaction_list_params.py) support financial_account, flow, created and limit. [Schema](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stripe/stripe-python/master/stripe/v2/money_management/_transaction.py) includes IDs, financial_account, flow IDs, pending/posted/void, balance impacts, fees and reversal categories. Schema evidence is not access entitlement. It does not establish complete coverage or joins for every Issuing authorization/capture/refund. Confirm card linkage and adjustment/fee coverage in the actual program; retain separate card reads.

**D/I:** [v2 overview](https://docs.stripe.com/api-v2-overview) uses page tokens and next_page_url/previous_page_url; lists are eventually consistent by default. Follow returned URLs with unchanged filters. The financial-account guide documents v2.money_management.transaction.created/updated, outbound payment/transfer created/posted/failed/returned/canceled/updated and received-credit available/failed/returned/succeeded events. These require current object/balance reads and overlap reconciliation, not assumptions of synchronized cash/card snapshots or freshness SLA.

**D/S:** Existing [v1 Treasury FinancialAccount](https://docs.stripe.com/api/treasury/financial_accounts/object?query=) is a different contract: GET /v1/treasury/financial_accounts/{id} has balance.cash (spendable now), inbound_pending/outbound_pending. [Transactions](https://docs.stripe.com/api/treasury/transactions/list): GET /v1/treasury/transactions?financial_account=... plus individual reads, open/posted/void, flow and balance impacts. [Entries](https://docs.stripe.com/api/treasury/transaction_entries/list): /v1/treasury/transaction_entries plus individual reads and effective balance changes. Both lists use v1 cursors. These establish surfaces, not new-program v1 eligibility. Do not mix v1 cash/status/events with v2 preview semantics; obtain the actual program version/event inventory.

**D/A:** [Funding guide](https://docs.stripe.com/treasury/connect/moving-money/fund-a-financial-account) documents payments-balance and external-bank flows with timing/ownership conditions. [Financial-account Issuing](https://docs.stripe.com/issuing/connect-v2) requires card capabilities/programs; requesting a capability alone does not create a program. Card-to-designated-account mapping, preview entitlement, refunds/fees/dispute balance impacts and permitted public-contribution funding remain unverified.

## Proposed allocator accounting (I; no implementation/numeric policy)

Bind nonprofit -> Slander campaign/Advertising fund -> provider account/context -> product balance/financial account -> card -> platform advertiser/billing account -> platform campaigns -> invoice/payment. Merchant labels and last four digits are insufficient. Record currency, product/version, observation/effective time, reconciliation coverage and discrepancies. One campaign per nonprofit simplifies mapping but does not designate every unrelated receipt in its Wallet as advertising money.

Maintain a reconciled provider ledger and separate obligations/allocation ledger. Proposed headroom: **provider spend-available cash minus obligations/reservations not already reflected in that cash**. This is a design rule, not a formula for arbitrary provider fields:

1. Start from the exact instrument's verified spend-available USD. Total raised, pending collections, inbound pending or collections awaiting transfer are not spendable card funds. Track unsettled contributions/transfers until destination availability is reconciled. Source debit and destination credit move the same money, not two funds.
2. Record holds/pending flows for reconciliation; subtract only portions not already excluded from availability. Stripe Issuing holds already reduce it. At capture, hold release and capture debit represent one purchase; do not subtract both plus the posted charge from a fresh balance.
3. Reserve incurred-but-unbilled Meta/TikTok liabilities using platform delivery/spend and billing evidence, including applicable taxes/fees, unpaid invoices and corrections. Card providers cannot show delivered services not yet authorized. Match obligations to card holds/captures/settlement; move coverage between local reservation and provider exclusion without duplicate subtraction or premature release.
4. Reserve new planned allocations atomically across workers/platforms sharing campaign headroom. Track uncertain budget writes and effective delivery state. A configured budget is not cash debited: distinguish unconsumed commitments from incurred spend, replacing reservations as they become liability/hold/payment.
5. Donor refunds/disputes and merchant card credits are different flows. Maintain collection-deficit/refund liquidity separately. Provisional credits/expected refunds are not reusable cash until verified and covered by policy. Card refunds do not automatically reverse donor refunds or cancel platform invoices; quarantine unmatched credits.
6. Record reconciliation boundaries; deduplicate events, fetch missing objects, exhaust pages, revisit unresolved states, compare opening cash + classified impacts with closing cash. Cross-system reads are not atomic. Stale, missing or inconsistent finance data must **not authorize new spend**. Freshness/completeness policy requires human definition.

Example without invented amounts: approval reduces provider availability; use that hold as coverage and release duplicate local coverage of the same platform liability. Capture releases the hold/debits cash, then mark matched liability paid. Expiry restores availability but may leave platform liability, requiring local coverage again. A decline, canceled card or paused advertiser does not undo delivered advertising. Restored cash is not proof of canceled debt.

**U:** Neither card ledger nor platform report alone establishes instantaneous complete liability. Reporting lag/corrections, delayed/forced captures, unidentified charges, fees, unsettled funding, provisional disputes and concurrent delivery leave exposure unknowable. Conservative reservations or withholding allocations are needed when coverage cannot be established. No numeric reserves, polling/freshness threshold or loss tolerance is invented. Final pause/escalation, existing-delivery recovery, restart, residual release and approval authority remain human decisions. Refusing new allocations does not prove existing ads paused.
