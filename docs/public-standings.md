# Public daily and weekly standings (T14)

Visitors open **Daily and weekly standings** from the public campaign navigation without an account. They choose a Chicago day or Monday–Sunday week and see the selected targets, ranked ads, provisional status, result update time, last successful paid import, source data-as-of and reporting delay. The page requests stored results every 30 seconds; this does not request providers or promise new measurements at that frequency. Overlapping requests are generation-fenced so a slower old response cannot replace a newer response.

The manager's **Standings** workspace publishes a stored provisional result through `standings:refresh`. The operation accepts only the selected campaign/period and an operation identity. Current manager membership and nonprofit scope are checked before both writes and exact retries. Each refresh records actor, source, time, version, settings identity, input fingerprint, outcome and snapshot identity. Immutable snapshots preserve earlier results. The visible history distinguishes stored, unchanged and held attempts. Publishing neither awards a prize nor initiates a financial action.

`paidWindowProjection` supplies the same effective paid authority used by T12/T13: exact manager certification can establish its own scope, uncertified fallback cannot establish completeness, stale or missing imports remain held, and a subday replacement does not invent the unknown remainder of a coarser day. Platform provenance is not another source of score inputs. No public caller supplies metrics or completeness.

Fundraising uses the shared trusted `fundraisingPeriodResults`, checked against the exact set of eligible confirmed donations in the half-open period and `currentCommission`'s refund-corrected eligible gross cents. Donation counts, commission amounts and estimated advertising conversions are not fundraising dollars. An unfinished or held latest refund read holds the whole result. Absence of donations is not an affirmative zero: an explicit trusted period record is required. The nonregistered T11 integration seam is the producer boundary; the run-owned `standingsZero` fixture exercises explicit empty-period evidence and cannot run in application deployments. New donations or refund evidence invalidate the old input fingerprint. Partial-period commission remains governed by the original T10/T11 earning rules and is never discarded by standings.

The score is `40*sqrt(min(impressions/target,1)) + 30*sqrt(min(linkClicks/target,1)) + 30*sqrt(min(fundraisingCents/targetCents,1))`. Targets are the campaign's locked positive daily or weekly targets. Twenty-five impressions, 36 link clicks and $64 against targets 100, 100 and $100 score 62. Whole-cent conversion is consistent on both sides of the fundraising ratio. Rank compares the unrounded score, then total link clicks, fundraising cents and impressions descending. Display rounding never determines rank. Exact remaining ties share competition rank (1, 1, 3); rank-one ties split one fixed prize, with remainder cents assigned by ascending entry creation time then stable entry ID. Each entry competes independently, including multiple ads by one creator and repeated daily/weekly wins.

Actual Chicago calendar boundaries define each half-open period, including 23/25-hour days and 167/169-hour weeks. Only periods wholly contained within the competition dates have prize proposals. Partial opening or closing periods display no prize and preserve otherwise-eligible commission. A missing input never becomes zero. If any required entry input is incomplete or changed since publication, public reads retain the entire last-known table with its original update time and **Some results are delayed**. Without a prior snapshot, no ranking is fabricated. Every public read revalidates current campaign, approved creative version and withdrawal/removal cutoff; hidden creative cannot leak through a historical snapshot, and surviving rows retain their historical ranks.

Public projections contain only entry links/public copy, aggregate metrics, score/rank, proposed prize shares and aggregate freshness. Private donor details, commission/payment records, provider account/ad/grant references, raw source evidence, operation fingerprints and credentials remain private. Manager refresh history is not public.

All outputs remain provisional. Actual provider metric/completeness/freshness and account gates under #12, actual fundraising/provider release gates under #20/#21, T15 prize funding, T16 finalization and later exceptional eligibility/correction owners remain required. The controlled evidence proves bounded application behavior and cannot establish actual provider acceptance or authorize awards, payments, advertising or deployment.
