## Public evidence and access limits

### Meta: fields can be identified, but measurement and coverage are not certified

**D, official code:** Meta's Python Business SDK declares `impressions` and `inline_link_clicks` as strings and `outbound_clicks` as a list of `AdsActionStats`. It exposes publisher, position, device and advertiser-time-zone hourly breakdown names. Its account Insights reader uses GET and supports `level`, `time_range`, `time_increment`, `filtering` and `breakdowns`. Account metadata includes `timezone_name`, `timezone_id` and `timezone_offset_hours_utc`. These are schema facts; enum membership does not establish that combinations work together or have complete coverage. Sources were also read directly over HTTPS at pinned revision `b08e5c1d36a2adad3c4ede6578654c8eaca5f225`: [Insights schema](https://github.com/facebook/facebook-python-business-sdk/blob/b08e5c1d36a2adad3c4ede6578654c8eaca5f225/facebook_business/adobjects/adsinsights.py), [account reader and metadata](https://github.com/facebook/facebook-python-business-sdk/blob/b08e5c1d36a2adad3c4ede6578654c8eaca5f225/facebook_business/adobjects/adaccount.py). Search-cache content differed from the directly retrieved SDK; the pinned code is the reproducible code reference.

**D, indexed first-party text only:** Meta's Business Suite help defines link clicks as clicks to advertiser-specified destinations on or off Meta technologies. The directly opened page exposed no readable body in this run. This establishes a useful semantic warning, not the precise `inline_link_clicks` API definition. [Meta performance help](https://z-p4-lookaside.fbsbx.com/business/help/1027487236056863).

**P:** The current [Insights overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/insights/), [breakdown guide](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/insights/breakdowns/) and [rate-limit guide](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/overview/rate-limiting/) returned HTTP 429. The [ads_read reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/permissions/reference/ads_read/) was inaccessible through the research tool. The [link-click help](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/674769555983979) and [outbound-click help](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/659185130844708) redirected to login/temporary-block pages. Their unseen contents are not evidence here. No numeric Meta rate budget, exact revision cutoff, maximum lookback, read-only scope sufficiency, or hourly DST behavior is asserted from them.

**D:** Meta's public Postman collection describes user/system-user tokens, app/account prerequisites, permission-dependent access and cursor pagination. Its broad own-account management example names both `ads_read` and `ads_management`; it does not prove that write permission is necessary for a reporting-only integration. It distinguishes Standard access for own-account management from Advanced access for others' accounts. [Meta collection](https://www.postman.com/meta/facebook-marketing-api/collection/0zr4mes/facebook-marketing-api-mapi).

**I/P/A:** `ads_read` without `ads_management` is the least-privilege reporting candidate, not a verified minimum in this investigation. Test account metadata, ad inventory and Insights separately. Do not add `business_management`, Instagram organic-insight scopes, Page scopes or write permission merely because a broad sample includes them. If inventory discovery needs another scope, isolate and document that requirement. App access level, business asset assignment and the authorizing person's account role are distinct from token scopes. Do not infer permission from a token being issued.

**I/P:** Meta link clicks and outbound clicks must remain separate candidates. The usual interpretation of outbound as leaving Meta still needs accessible current authoritative confirmation for this API/version and the eligible formats. If total link clicks includes on-platform destinations, replacing it with outbound clicks changes scoring. Even for website-only creative, equality of the two observed numbers does not establish permanent equivalence. Preserve both raw fields; never add them together. Clicks (all), unique clicks and landing-page views do not establish the PRD's total link-click measure.

### TikTok: v1.3 evidence is useful but v2 is a distinct contract

**D:** TikTok defines impressions as ad displays, destination clicks as clicks to the specified destination, and all clicks as including social interactions. [TikTok basic metrics](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/basic-data?lang=en). Its indexed v1.3 compatibility table maps `clicks` to destination clicks, separates `gross_impressions` including invalid impressions, and makes dimension/mode availability conditional. It gives `<5` as a possible impressions value with search terms. [v1.3 dimension table](https://business-api.tiktok.com/portal/docs/supported-metrics-for-a-dimension-in-basic-reports/v1.3). Neither source proves the v2 ad-level field semantics for every format.

**D, indexed first-party endpoint text:** v2 merges basic/audience/DSA reporting; removes `report_type`, `data_level` and singular `advertiser_id`; uses plural advertiser IDs and plural filter values. Smart+ `ad_id` can identify an asset group rather than the former creative. Warnings move from the v1.3 throttle header into `extra_info`. The synchronous reference says POST/JSON but its example says GET. Dates are account-local and inclusive; `query_in_utc0` exists. Limits by time grain are hour: one day, day: 30 days, week: 180 days, otherwise 365 days. A 20,000-ad cutoff requires ID partitioning (at most 100 filter IDs); totals can also be truncated. [v2 synchronous reference](https://business-api.tiktok.com/portal/docs/run-a-synchronous-report/v2.0).

The direct TikTok portal opens initially returned no readable lines; targeted searches retrieved substantial indexed provider text, including that contradiction. This is accessible indexed documentation, not an account response or proof of what the live endpoint accepts. The provider's [v2 guide](https://business-api.tiktok.com/portal/docs?id=1776454619412481) describes incremental documentation updates, but that does not certify arbitrary v1.3/v2 combinations.

**D:** The v2 API reference lists `Reporting > Consolidated Report` permission for reporting. [v2 endpoint/scope overview](https://business-api.tiktok.com/portal/docs/api-reference/v2.0). **P/A:** establish the exact granted permission, advertiser role, app approval, token lifecycle and account inventory read permissions for the selected authorization flow. Do not carry forward the older note's non-expiring-token statement as a universal lifecycle rule: the v2 overview also lists a short-term-token refresh endpoint.

**D:** Async task creation allows 1 QPS per app and 500 creations per ad account per token user per hour. Data refreshes every several hours and current-day data can be inaccurate; compatible dimensions differ by mode. Async dates use account time zones, no pagination is provided, maximum range is 365 days, tasks expire after 30 days, and translated titles can be disabled. [v2 async creation](https://business-api.tiktok.com/portal/docs/create-an-asynchronous-report-task/v2.0). These task-creation limits are not synchronous-read, task-status, download or metadata budgets.

**P/A:** obtain an authoritative supported v2 request and metric/dimension table, confirm the canonical method, and test version/format identity. Do not implement an automatic POST-to-GET or v2-to-v1.3 fallback: it could disguise an incompatible response contract. A method that happens to work is account evidence; provider confirmation is still needed to choose the supported production method. TikTok's synchronous reference explicitly recommends comparing Ads Manager downloads to async output rather than assuming synchronous parity. That distinction must be reflected in validation.

### Freshness, backfills and finality

**D:** TikTok's v1.3 latency guide describes approximately 30-minute real-time reporting for qualifying basic fields/dimensions, with extreme delays of two hours or more. Real-time data is adjusted the next day; it recommends fetching after a 12:00 UTC daily correction. Certain dimensions/UV fields use offline data with typically 16-hour latency. The guide describes offline processing as complete with no further modifications needed; it does not establish that every chosen v2 response is that offline dataset or provide this campaign's version-specific completeness watermark. [TikTok latency guide](https://business-api.tiktok.com/portal/docs/data-latency-for-reports/v1.3).

**I/P/A:** verify how those latency categories carry into the exact v2 report. Do not turn the offline description into a universal guarantee that every historical value is permanently immutable. No accessible Meta freshness/finality SLA was established. For both providers, distinguish maximum query span from historical retention/lookback and from a promised correction horizon. The last two remain unanswered for the actual contract.

An investigation-only backfill schedule is: re-read each test period near end, end+2h, +24h, +48h, +72h and +7d; for TikTok also capture the first run after the relevant 12:00 UTC correction. Repeat older approved reference periods and compare revisions. These are observation checkpoints, not an adopted production polling policy or proof of finality. Preserve each snapshot and correction difference. Query overlapping windows as replacements of matching facts, not incremental counts.

The PRD's 30-second public refresh and five-minute import-outage threshold concern different clocks from provider measurement freshness. An unchanged successful import can be healthy while all values remain provisional. Rate constraints must be capacity-tested with backfills, pagination, failures and metadata reads included; public refresh does not justify 30-second provider polling.

## Coverage and Chicago time contract

The following is a proposed evidence model, not a provider-issued completion certificate.

For every required **competition entry × provider account × paid source/placement × metric × Chicago period**, retain the expected-source inventory and effective mapping dates; API/report version and ad/creative/asset identity; named account zone and query-zone options; exact requested and effective UTC bounds; grain, dimensions, filters and reporting settings; raw snapshots and request IDs; fetch time; all page/file manifests; warnings and reconciliation outcome. Record import-operation outcome separately from coverage status and measurement freshness.

Create expected inventory independently of report rows: an entry known to have placements cannot disappear from expectations because its metrics response is empty. Include paused, archived/deleted and no-delivery objects through their historical eligibility; verify provider retrieval behavior. One Meta ad can deliver on Facebook and Instagram. Partition its publisher rows once; do not also add the ad total, duplicated creative reports or a separate Instagram import of the same delivery. TikTok non-TikTok placements and Meta other publishers require explicit eligibility mapping; unknown publisher values remain unresolved rather than silently included or dropped.

Use [REQ-116](PRD-adoption-is-launch.md) as authority for inclusive start/exclusive end, full local days, Monday-Sunday weeks and actual DST boundaries. Derived test fixtures for America/Chicago in 2026 are:

| Chicago period | Expected UTC interval, exclusive end | Elapsed length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spring change day, March 8 | `[2026-03-08T06:00Z, 2026-03-09T05:00Z)` | 23h |
| Fall change day, November 1 | `[2026-11-01T05:00Z, 2026-11-02T06:00Z)` | 25h |
| Week March 2 through March 8 | `[2026-03-02T06:00Z, 2026-03-09T05:00Z)` | 167h |
| Week October 26 through November 1 | `[2026-10-26T05:00Z, 2026-11-02T06:00Z)` | 169h |

These are boundary expectations to check with a recorded IANA time-zone database version, not provider observations. Both fall 01:00 hours must be accounted for; spring 02:00 does not occur. A provider may aggregate repeated hours into one bucket: require a documented mapping that preserves counts, not necessarily 25 rows. Zero-suppressed hours likewise need coverage evidence.

Local arithmetic check performed during this research: Windows `.NET TimeZoneInfo` with `Central Standard Time` reproduced all four UTC intervals and the 23/25/167/169-hour lengths above. No Python/IANA-library check was completed. This arithmetic check does not validate either provider's buckets.

If account daily boundaries equal Chicago's historical boundaries, validated daily reporting may suffice. A fixed UTC-6 zone is not interchangeable with America/Chicago. For other account zones, validate hourly reconstruction using unambiguous intervals, all surrounding source dates and exact filters. UTC hourly data can be rebucketed only when hour identities and counts are complete. Daily UTC totals alone cannot be split exactly into Chicago days. Non-hour-aligned account offsets can make hourly data insufficient too. Do not prorate counts or relabel a date. If the needed granular report is unavailable, report that concrete limitation rather than changing policy.

Coverage may be considered established only after a provider-backed rule explains all expected sources, the full interval, required metrics, legitimate no-row/zero behavior, truncation, suppression and applicable processing stage. It is separate from an assertion that values can never change. A successful, fully processed import with unknown measurement coverage can reset REQ-126's clock while REQ-117 still holds finalization. A failed page, failed parsing or failed file download means the import operation itself failed. Neither an unchanged value nor the passage of 48 hours supplies missing evidence.
