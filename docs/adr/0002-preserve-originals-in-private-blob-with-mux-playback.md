# Preserve original videos in private Vercel Blob and stream through Mux

On 2026-11-11, Q98 adopted private Vercel Blob for exact original video files, Mux for processed streaming, and Convex for permissions, media records and processing state. Mux does not retain the exact uploaded original, while Convex's two-minute direct-upload timeout is a poor fit for reliable uploads at the accepted 5,000,000,000-byte maximum; the selected split preserves original bytes independently of playback while adding separate original-storage and Mux usage costs. The [Q97 investigation](../provider-research-adoption-is-launch.md#q97-original-video-preservation-investigation--2026-11-11) records provider evidence and the proposed upload/ingestion flow.

Creators upload once directly to private Blob storage through a scoped multipart upload. Convex records the original's owner, submission/revision, storage identifier, filename, size, integrity/validation state and associated Mux identifiers. Mux ingests through temporary source access, and verified processing events update Convex records. Public playback and authorized original-file downloads are separate; changing or deleting a Mux playback asset does not itself delete its archived source.

Q100 permits original-file downloads by the uploading creator, that campaign's manager and authorized platform administrators. Other creators and public visitors have no access to the archived original. Clerk authenticates the caller; Convex enforces current role and resource scope before granting temporary source-file access.

Q101 retains originals after campaign completion or ad withdrawal, with no automatic expiry. Deletion requires an explicit administrator action; removing public playback leaves the original intact.

Q102 blocks original-file deletion while the ad is active or being published. Once the ad is no longer in use, an administrator must explicitly confirm deletion; preserve an audit record of that action. Removing a public ad alone is not an archive-deletion request.

Q103 provides a 30-day recovery period after the administrator's confirmed deletion request. Retain the original bytes during that period, with storage usage continuing; permanent Blob deletion happens only after the recovery period. Restoring an original during the window cancels its pending purge. Implementation must enforce the active/publishing safeguard and reconcile restore/purge races without claiming physical deletion before it is confirmed. The audit record survives deletion.

Q104 confirmed the selected architecture and these access/retention/deletion policies with "Yes that sounds good," completing shared understanding for the correction. Large-file integrity/validation execution, the exact Convex verification harness and provider integration behavior still require engineering validation. The recovery window does not establish a separate backup or protection guarantee against provider/data loss. Q99 clarified that Clerk remains responsible for applicable application authentication, with Convex enforcing current permissions; see [ADR-0001](0001-convex-vercel-launch-stack.md). No account setup, implementation, publication or deployment is authorized by this design confirmation, and no provider-dashboard login was performed.
