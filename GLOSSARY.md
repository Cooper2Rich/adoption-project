# adoption.is Campaign

Shared language for the adoption.is advertising campaign and its creative contributors.

## Language

**Creator**:
An invited participant who contributes advertising creative for the adoption.is campaign.
_Avoid_: Donor, campaign owner

**Tracked fundraising link**:
A link unique to an ad that identifies the ad to which resulting confirmed fundraising is attributed. It is distinct from a creator sign-in magic link.
_Avoid_: Fundraising magic link, sign-in link

**Campaign manager**:
The person operating a nonprofit's campaign, reviewing ads, setting advertising controls and approving creator payments.

**Platform administrator**:
The platform operator who approves campaign ad-capacity expansion and reviews refund requests initiated by a campaign manager.

**Advertiser account**:
A nonprofit-owned account on an advertising platform used to operate its paid placements, distinct from its Slander campaign and its financial-provider account.
_Avoid_: Advertising fund, developer application

**Developer application**:
Slander's provider-registered application through which it receives authorized delegated access to nonprofit advertising assets.
_Avoid_: Advertiser account, nonprofit identity

**Competition entry**:
An ad competing for campaign prizes, with its own creative and tracked fundraising link. Creator corrections before first approval retain the entry; replacement creative after approval creates a new entry. Descriptive-only edits and unchanged creative running across several paid-ad platforms do not create new entries.

**Original video**:
The exact, unmodified video file uploaded by a creator for a competition entry or its revision. It is distinct from processed playback versions and reconstructed downloadable masters.

**Withdrawal**:
An end to a competition entry's future participation that preserves its history and eligibility for activity before withdrawal. Its tracked fundraising link continues supporting adoption.is without commission or competition credit for later activity.

**Certified metric correction**:
A manager-approved, evidence-backed replacement for a reported metric of one competition entry over a specified time range. It remains authoritative for that scope until the campaign manager releases it.

**Disqualification**:
A campaign manager's decision that selected activity of a competition entry is ineligible for selected dates. It is distinct from removal that stops future participation while preserving valid past earnings.

**Finalized prize correction**:
A campaign manager's explicit revision of a finalized winner or prize amount that preserves the original result and records the correction separately.

**Paid prize loss**:
An erroneous prize payment retained as a campaign loss after a finalized prize correction. It is distinct from the prize owed to the correct creator and from a fundraising commission reversal.

**Fundraising commission**:
Creator earnings calculated from eligible fundraising attributed to their ad, distinct from a competition prize.

**Prize fund**:
Money allocated to the current campaign's fixed competition prizes, distinct from advertising funding and reserved creator earnings.

**Advertising fund**:
Money designated to pay for campaign advertising, primarily contributed through public advertising crowdfunding and supplemented by client contributions. It is distinct from fundraising donations, creator earnings and the prize fund.
_Avoid_: Ad account, prize fund, fundraising donations

**Advertising contribution**:
Money contributed to a selected nonprofit campaign's advertising fund, allocated by campaign baseline-and-boost rules rather than earmarked for a particular ad. The contribution itself earns no creator commission, prize-fund replenishment or fundraising score credit.
_Avoid_: Fundraising donation, creator payout

**Advertising contribution refund**:
A return of money from an advertising contribution to its original contributor, distinct from returning campaign funds to the nonprofit.

**Campaign balance return**:
A return of unused campaign advertising funds to the same nonprofit after paid-campaign closure and reconciliation, distinct from refunding an individual contributor.
_Avoid_: Advertising contribution refund, creator payout

**Disputed advertising contribution**:
An advertising contribution subject to a provider dispute whose affected amount is excluded from spendable campaign funds while unresolved.

**Fundraising donation**:
Money given to support the nonprofit's fundraising purpose, distinct from an advertising contribution. Qualifying tracked-link donations can earn creator commission and fundraising score credit under the campaign's attribution rules.

**Boosted ad**:
A competition entry selected to receive extra advertising spend in addition to its baseline allocation. The campaign manager chooses a target top-entry count using the current daily leaderboard, with entries tied at the cutoff included.
_Avoid_: Prize winner, paid prize

**Baseline advertising allocation**:
The daily advertising amount set by the campaign manager for each eligible funded creative, including its initial trial, prorated for admission partway through a Chicago calendar day. It is distinct from additional boost spending and from money paid to its creator.
_Avoid_: Creator payout, competition prize

**Advertising continuation minimum**:
The nonprofit-defined performance threshold, with nonprofit-selected contribution weights, an ad must meet to continue receiving campaign advertising spend. It applies after the initial trial and in subsequent daily evaluations; failure ends paid spending while the creative remains publicly viewable on Slander.
_Avoid_: Disqualification, withdrawal

**Advertising performance policy**:
The continuation weights, metric targets, passing score and trial duration bound to an ad from the published policy current at actual paid admission. It governs the initial trial and later evaluations; queue or retrial authorization alone does not fix the policy version.

**Initial advertising trial**:
The manager-configured period of baseline paid advertising before an ad's first evaluation against the advertising continuation minimum. It starts at first confirmed paid delivery and excludes confirmed pauses; each trial day is 24 elapsed hours.

**Waiting approved creative**:
A publicly viewable, manager-approved creative awaiting paid admission because capacity, funding or another required readiness condition is unavailable. Waiting does not start its initial advertising trial.

**Advertising admission queue**:
The shared waiting order for new ads and retrials, based on first approval time for new ads and retrial-authorization time for retrials. Temporarily blocked entries retain their position while eligible entries may pass them for admission.

**Campaign ad capacity**:
The authorized number of campaign-funded ad slots, including initial trials, temporarily paused or provider-interrupted reservations and ads still stopping, distinct from creator count and total public ads. A cut ad releases its slot once all relevant paid placements are confirmed stopped; capacity can expand during the campaign.
_Avoid_: Creator count, lifetime submission limit

**Stopping advertising**:
The state of an ad whose new spending allocations have stopped while provider confirmation that every relevant paid placement has stopped remains outstanding. Its funded slot remains occupied.

**Advertising retrial**:
A new paid trial authorized by the campaign manager for an ad previously cut from advertising spend, subject to available capacity and funding. It preserves the history of earlier trials and cutoffs.

**Boost pool**:
The share of remaining allocatable daily advertising funds designated for selected top entries after baseline funding has been reserved. Its percentage is set by the campaign manager.

**Allocation preview**:
An advisory calculation of possible campaign advertising allocations from specified settings and evidence. Saving it does not reserve advertising funds or ad slots.

**Advertising goal**:
The campaign manager's selected intent for paid delivery: awareness or visits to an ad's tracked fundraising page. It is distinct from the public competition score and the advertising continuation minimum.

**Provider spending split**:
The campaign manager's percentage allocation between Meta advertising across Facebook/Instagram and TikTok, applied to each funded ad's baseline and boost. The shares total 100%, and either provider may receive 0%.

**Baseline trial reserve**:
Funding protected for an ad's entire initial baseline advertising trial, distinct from discretionary boosts and coverage for already-incurred obligations.

**Funded campaign run**:
The advertised period and open ad slots for which baseline advertising is covered before paid launch. Its baseline funding depends on the daily per-ad amount, funded days and open slots, and must permit completion of each initial trial.

**Paid campaign launch**:
The start of a campaign's paid advertising and competition, distinct from prelaunch setup, creator intake, crowdfunding and public display of waiting approved creatives.

**Financial safety pause**:
A temporary advertising pause caused by unreliable required financial evidence or insufficient funding for baseline commitments and obligations, distinct from an ad failing its performance minimum.

**Financial safety policy**:
Administrator-approved reserves, financial-evidence freshness limits and held-fund release conditions established from provider requirements and validation evidence.

**Launch blocker**:
An unmet required condition preventing paid campaign launch, with a recorded reason and resolution status.
