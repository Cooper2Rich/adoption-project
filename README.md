A responsive, provider-free local campaign marketplace demo built with React, Express and SQLite. It has three selectable workspaces: **Admin**, **Campaign Owner**, and **Creator**. Role selection models scope; it is not authentication.

## Run

Use Node.js **24 or later** (built-in SQLite).

```sh
npm install
npm run build
npm start
```

Open [the campaign demo](http://localhost:3000/campaigns). Development: `npm run dev`.

## Take the working demo journey

1. In Admin view, open **Campaigns** and choose **Initialize fictional showcase**. This explicit, idempotent action appends one fictional campaign, organization, creator and $1,000 of restricted simulated funding. It preserves existing records and never resets the workspace.
2. Switch to **Creator**, choose **Alex River · fictional**, and open the Riverkind campaign in Campaigns.
3. Read and **accept campaign terms**, then **submit sample creative**. The supplied image, rights and disclosures pass deterministic automatic eligibility. No owner sign-off is required.
4. **Create tracked giving link**, then **open tracked giving page**. Confirm a simulated **$100** gift. The shared journal confirms **$10 creator / $90 nonprofit before fixture fees**, plus **one eligible gift vote**. No card details or money movement.
5. Switch to **Campaign Owner**, select **Riverkind Collective · fictional**, and inspect Funding and Performance. Seed ($600) and media ($400) are separate from public gifts. The 77.5 grading-v2 illustration is labeled and separate from campaign results.
6. In **Admin**, open Settlement and **Preview & export sample week**. This exports a JSON statement from the shared settlement engine. The current sample week has not reached cutoff; no batch is approved and no creator is paid.

Original projects, submissions, private uploads, immutable revisions, automatic eligibility evidence, and scoped exception handling remain available through the existing navigation. Legacy `nonprofit` browser preferences are mapped to the Campaign Owner API view.

## Scope and limits

This is a clickable local demo, not a claim of complete PRD 2.2 implementation. Working shared domain modules cover campaign terms/consent/distributions, first-party attribution, simulated contributions and balanced accounting, refunds/replay protection, restricted pools and bounded reservations, weekly settlement/exports, economics, and grading-v2. The visible journey intentionally uses a small coherent slice of those APIs.

The showcase supplies fictional settled funding and sample media. Reports do not invent platform performance: missing denominators show N/A; the actual grade is provisional without evidence. The **77.5** example uses shared `gradeComponents` with fixed weights CTR 15 / views 10 / votes 35 / amount 40. It cannot award money or buy ads. The $25 floor / $10 commission / $15 top-up text is illustrative; initialization creates no guarantee promise.

Live payments, payouts, OAuth, social publishing, ad delivery, production identity verification, external metric ingestion and production backend deployment are unavailable. Advanced grading configuration, exposure scheduling/opportunity verification and settlement administration remain API previews or deferred UI. Disabled live controls are labeled. [Full demo capabilities and API notes](docs/DEMO.md).

## Data and verification

Within each campaign budget breakdown, **Ad budget breakdown** shows every ad's total and per-platform Allocated, Spent and Left amounts. Allocations are calculated as equal whole-cent shares of each platform budget among currently eligible ads; they are not separate manually saved ad budgets. Meta video-view targets and TikTok use video creatives. Completed sample spend joins by stable ad ID, so renamed, withdrawn or historical ads retain their spending. Ineligible ads receive no new allocation, overruns remain negative, and budgets with no eligible ad are shown as unassigned. Ad rows plus unassigned allocation reconcile to campaign totals. Previews respect the remaining per-ad budgets and may redistribute capped ad shares within the same platform, never across platforms.

Each campaign has a **Campaign budget breakdown** on its overview, Ad platforms and scoped Ad spending pages. It shows total allocated, spent and budget left, plus the same figures for Meta and TikTok. Allocation uses the saved campaign cap and platform percentage; left equals allocated minus included completed spend. Negative balances remain visible as overruns. Saving new rules updates the overview immediately and survives refresh. Include sample activity controls whether historical examples count toward spent and remaining amounts. Preview allocations respect both the remaining campaign budget and each platform's remainder, without transferring unused shares automatically or consuming money.

The campaign overview now includes **Set ad spend allocation**, an expandable form that saves the same daily limit, campaign cap and Meta/TikTok split used by Ad platforms without leaving the campaign. **Completed ad spend** appears on campaign overviews, Ad spending and Ad platforms. Fixed historical examples total **$700 for Coffee**, **$2,000 for River** and **$1,500 for Adoption**, with platform, creative, completion date, amount and Completed · sample labels. Include sample activity hides or shows the examples and remembers the choice. New campaigns have empty histories; archived campaigns retain their examples. These illustrations are separate from real platform spend, contributor allocations, creator earnings. Including the examples reduces the displayed budget left and limits allocation previews, without changing the saved cap.

Admin can open **Ad platforms** on any campaign card or in a campaign's secondary sidebar. The Meta and TikTok demo dialogs select a fictional USD ad account and target campaign. Configure a daily limit, campaign cap and platform split, enable demo automation, save, then **Preview next allocation**. The preview divides one daily planning budget equally among currently eligible creatives; TikTok and video-view targets use videos only. Unusable shares stay unallocated. Connections, rules and the latest preview persist separately per campaign in this browser. Changing or disconnecting a destination and archiving a campaign pause demo automation. This is the agreed demo setup: there is no live OAuth, scheduled worker, platform budget verification, ad purchase or actual-spend entry. Previews never debit contributions or consume a cumulative budget.
